Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Amber is a study app for the UK car theory test, made by Obed.

The short version

Amber has no accounts, no adverts and no analytics. Your progress stays on your iPhone. We don't collect, see or sell any information about you.

What Amber stores, and where

Your name (if you type one), your test date, your answers, scores, streaks and settings are saved in a file on your iPhone. A picture you choose for the league is saved there too. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it. You can also wipe it at any time with Settings → Reset progress.

Microphone and speech

If you turn on Answer by voice, or ask Amber a question by voice, Amber uses your microphone and Apple's speech recognition to turn what you say into text. On iPhones that support it, this happens on the phone. On iPhones that don't, Apple's speech recognition may send the audio to Apple to process, under Apple's privacy policy. Amber doesn't record or keep the audio. Voice features are off until you turn them on, and iOS asks your permission first.

Ask Amber

Questions you type or speak in Ask Amber are answered on your iPhone, using Amber's own study material and, where available, Apple Intelligence running on the device. They are not sent to us.

Reminders

If you turn on study reminders, they are scheduled on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to a server.

Links

Settings has links to GOV.UK pages (booking a test, the Highway Code, Know Your Traffic Signs). Opening one takes you to that website, which has its own privacy policy.

Children

Amber is suitable for learner drivers of any age. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.
