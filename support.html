Get in touch

Email obed.richman@gmail.com with questions, problems or a mistake you have spotted in a question. It helps to say which iPhone you have and what you were doing.

Common questions

Is this the official DVSA material?
No. Amber is an independent study aid. Its questions are original, written from the Highway Code, and its hazard clips are animated practice scenes. It is worth trying the official DVSA practice test and hazard perception clips as well before your test.

Where is my progress kept?
On your iPhone only. There is no account, so progress does not move to a new phone, and deleting the app deletes it.

How do I start again?
Settings, then Reset progress.

Voice answers aren't working.
Check that Amber is allowed to use the microphone and speech recognition: iPhone Settings, then Amber.

Ask Amber only shows study material, with no written answer.
Written answers need Apple Intelligence, which is on newer iPhones and must be switched on in iPhone Settings.

Which iPhones does it work on?
iPhones running iOS 26 or later.
