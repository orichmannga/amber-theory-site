Amber: Driving Theory UK

Pass your theory. Then go.

Amber is an iPhone app for revising the UK car theory test: a daily plan built around your test date, over 900 original questions written from the Highway Code, every road sign, and animated 3D hazard perception clips.

Amber is an independent study aid. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the DVSA, and its questions and clips are original practice material.
